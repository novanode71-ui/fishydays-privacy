# FishyDays Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 12, 2026**

FishyDays is a single-player fishing game developed by Novanode.

## Data Collection

FishyDays does not collect, transmit, track, or share any personal information.

## Local Data

Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device. No gameplay data is sent to Novanode or external servers.

## Third-Party Services

FishyDays is available through Microsoft Store and itch.io. These platforms have their own privacy policies and data practices.
